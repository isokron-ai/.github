# Security policy

This policy covers repositories in the IsoKron organization.

## Reporting a vulnerability in our code

Please email **hello@isokron.ai** with "Security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.
Include the repository, version or commit, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see.
We acknowledge reports within five business days and keep you updated until the issue is resolved.

## Repositories based on someone else's project

Some repositories here are research builds of other projects (for example
`github-mcp-writepath-experiment`, based on `github/github-mcp-server`). A vulnerability in the
upstream project should go to that project's own security process, not to us.
